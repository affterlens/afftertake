The Creator
Behind the Lens

Welcome to Afftertake. I am the visual artist and admin behind this platform, based out of Jalgaon, Maharashtra. My journey in visual media started with a passion for capturing raw emotions and dynamic moments.

While smartphone cameras offer convenience, I realized that true cinematic excellence requires dedicated, professional gear. That is why I transitioned exclusively to the DJI Osmo Pocket 3.

The upgrade to a 1-inch sensor shooting in 10-bit D-Log M completely transformed my workflow. It allows me to extract maximum dynamic range and apply rich, high-end color grading that smartphones simply cannot replicate. Combined with a mechanical 3-axis gimbal, the stabilization is buttery smooth, ensuring every frame feels like a scene from a movie.

Automotive &
Car Deliveries

Taking delivery of a new vehicle is a monumental occasion that deserves to be documented in style.

The DJI Osmo's mechanical gimbal is a game-changer for automotive cinematography. It enables me to execute perfectly smooth tracking shots, sweeping curves around the vehicle's bodylines, and dramatic reveal angles without a single jitter. The result is a high-end, commercial-style video that makes your new car look its absolute best.
